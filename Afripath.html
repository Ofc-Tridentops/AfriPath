The problem

The experience trap is locking young people out.

Young people face systemic barriers to entering the workforce. Even when opportunities exist, they are blocked by key gaps:

  • Where to find verified opportunities
  • Knowing whether they qualify for available roles
  • Which skills they need to close the qualification gap
  • How to gain experience without having a first job
  • Direct, transparent access to employers

The cycle we break

Millions of talented young people are stuck in this loop:

🚫 No job
↓
📄 No experience
↓
🔁 No experience → can't get a job
AfriPath connects all four pieces — opportunities, skills, experience and employers — into one continuous pathway.
Who We Are

Bridging the gap between potential and employment.

AfriPath was built to rewrite the narrative for Africa's young talent through integrated pathways, skill building, and transparent opportunities.

🌍

What AfriPath Is About

AfriPath is a pan-African ecosystem designed to give every young person direct, equal access to career pathways. We unify job searching, targeted skill assessment, practical project work, verified experience and employer connections.

🎯

Our Goal

To eliminate the “no experience, no job” trap across Africa. We aim to equip 1 million young leaders with verified skill profiles, hands-on portfolio projects and direct connections to employers.

🚀

Our Mission Statement

To empower Africa’s youth by transforming raw talent into verified expertise, bridging skill gaps through actionable learning, and opening direct pathways to sustainable, dignified employment.
